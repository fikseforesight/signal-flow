## Doubts

- **The item itself is thinner than it looks.** One trade survey (Becker's Hospital Review), self-reported by nurse leaders, no independent verification of the 71%-to-41% numbers, no detail on sample size or methodology. "CNOs bet on home health" is a punchier headline than the underlying data supports — it's really a story about plans slipping, which happens in every sector, every year, for reasons (budget, staffing, logistics) not stated here.
- Treat the implied trend — "care is moving into homes" — as already well-known and not news; what's actually new is only the execution gap, which is a much smaller and more uncertain claim.
